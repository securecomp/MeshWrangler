AGL is calculated automatically when online terrain elevations are available. A reported altitude of zero is unreported; an assumed 6 ft AGL is available for RF modeling only and is not counted as an AGL observation. Filters apply to historical position records and their associated nodes, including the map and RF endpoints. Aircraft candidates are screening flags, not verified aircraft. Fixed nodes with repeated locations are excluded from moving candidates; AGL ≥ 100 ft without credible movement is unresolved; derived speeds require distinct dated positions and do not represent packet measurements. Altitude is MSL, not above terrain. *Speed uses documented protobuf ground_speed in km/h; the one-time audit compares both unit interpretations against raw records.
